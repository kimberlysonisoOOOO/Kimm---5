ESP32 WiFi Manager

Configure the WiFi connection for your ESP32.
How it works:
1. Enter your WiFi SSID and password.
2. Click Save & Connect.
3. ESP32 saves the settings.
4. ESP32 restarts.
5. ESP32 connects to your WiFi.
6. Open the ESP32 IP address to access the monitor.
